# PEACHES source cards

These cards are independent reading notes retained with the pre-Genesis source
package. They preserve source identity, version, candidate role, nonclaims,
bounded tests, and open burdens. They do not copy source text, adopt a source,
establish conformance, define [PEACHES Floor v1](FLOOR.md), create Genesis, or
issue a stamp. [PREGENESIS-FREEZE.md](PREGENESIS-FREEZE.md) records the exact
current boundary.

## PAL v2.3 family

- **Identity:** Spine 2.3, Compatibility Note 2.3-C, Ledger 2.3-L, Mathematical Atlas 2.3-M, Conformance Tests 2.3-T; public record [PAL](https://zenodo.org/records/22240134).
- **Candidate role:** trace-bearing cuts, receipts, obligations, residuals, reopening, version/branch continuity, and claim ceilings.
- **Kept:** account identity, origin cut, ordered receipts, immutable corrections, explicit transitions, open obligations, stable residual identifiers, and local closure.
- **Nonclaims:** PAL is not a complete ontology, consciousness result, ethical authority, or universal equation. A hash or passing bounded test is evidence only.
- **Offline check:** validate a finite envelope and receipt history; report unsupported native channels as residuals.
- **Open burden:** D-FIRST-OCCURRENCE and multi-parent lineage remain open. PPP/PEA/PECAN references to earlier PAL versions require explicit compatibility handling.

## PECAN v1.0.4

- **Public locator:** [PECAN](https://zenodo.org/records/21760884).
- **Candidate role:** consequential-crossing vocabulary and authority ceiling.
- **Kept:** description, recommendation, permission, and authorization are separate typed states; a receipt never upgrades one into another.
- **Nonclaims:** PECAN does not grant consent, standing, jurisdiction, or authority and does not execute an action.
- **Offline check:** compare a separately supplied grant to subject/object/action/scope/time/revocation/remaining use, returning unresolved when the basis is missing.
- **Open burden:** no live institutional grant or policy is embedded in the book.

## PEA Core v1.1.3

- **Public locator:** [PEA Core](https://zenodo.org/records/21911684).
- **Candidate role:** bounded external review of ethics, authority, consent, privacy, reversibility, remedy, contest, refusal, and human responsibility.
- **Kept:** evaluator, purpose, affected people, sourced reasons, uncertainty, conditions, and human route remain visible.
- **Nonclaims:** a review candidate is not a decision, permission, institutional designation, or execution instruction.
- **Offline check:** explain only supplied, source-linked reasons; never invent a human judgment.
- **Open burden:** an evaluator grant and institution-facing profile are deferred.

## SEED v0.3

- **Public locator:** [SEED](https://zenodo.org/records/21760893).
- **Candidate role:** human-facing release discipline.
- **Kept:** claim status, limits, uncertainty, privacy, correction, reopening, refusal, natural stop, and space for human choice.
- **Nonclaims:** no model personhood, need, harm, warmth, or agency is inferred from output.
- **Offline check:** require an explicit purpose, audience, privacy boundary, correction path, stop rule, and open choice list.
- **Open burden:** product-specific release policy remains outside this record.

## CHARTER v1.0

- **Public locator:** [CHARTER](https://zenodo.org/records/22288471).
- **Candidate role:** bounded carriage, selection, finite obligations, evidence, verifier, ledger state, residuals, and reopening.
- **Kept:** object/witness/certificate/evidence/receipt/verifier remain distinct; selected carry is explicit and scoped.
- **Nonclaims:** prime-shell mathematics, persistence language, roles, or a task carrier do not create liveness, consciousness, authority, or a book identity.
- **Offline check:** verify finite work limits, required evidence, distinct obligations, and selected-carry references.
- **Open burden:** no prime-shell theorem is needed to operate the minimal book.

## PPP Kernel v0.6

- **Public locator:** source identity is retained in `FOUNDING-MANIFEST.json`.
- **Candidate role:** optional typed integration of PAL, PECAN, PEA, and SEED.
- **Kept:** compatibility is a named mapping with residuals.
- **Nonclaims:** its historical PAL v2.2 manifest is not silently relabeled PAL v2.3 and PPP is not an irreducible PEACHES dependency.
- **Offline check:** record a compatibility disposition and source version; do not claim blanket conformance.

## Reading disposition

The pre-Genesis package keeps the five foundation families above as
source-linked references, not as an authority stack. `FLOOR.md` alone defines
the current minimal floor. The checker-signed SQLite implementation is a
separate nonnormative Branchline offline test-book profile; none of these
sources makes it an official stamper, checker, registry, or Book service.

Math, science, context, software, BRIDGE, GOLD, GPPR, and related research
remain optional shelves for Hearthline/toolkit work; they are not hidden
prerequisites of the public provenance floor.
